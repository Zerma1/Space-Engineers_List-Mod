[h1]Zerma - Docking Light[/h1]

A spotlight for large and small grids that projects a [b]line of glowing globes[/b] along its beam, to guide ships to a docking port.

[table]
[tr][td][b]Version[/b][/td][td]1.0[/td][/tr]
[tr][td][b]Tested on Space Engineers[/b][/td][td]not tested in game yet[/td][/tr]
[tr][td][b]Last update[/b][/td][td]2026-11-15[/td][/tr]
[/table]

[h2]Description[/h2]
[list]
[*]Adjustable from the terminal: distance, spacing, globe size, vertical and horizontal offset.
[*]Blinking: none, global, outgoing wave or incoming wave, with period, on-time / wave width and minimum level.
[*]Toolbar actions to increase / decrease distance, spacing and offsets, with a selectable step.
[*]Power use grows with the number of globes (up to 100 per large lamp, 50 per small lamp).
[*]Settings are saved in the block and synced in multiplayer.
[/list]

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Unlocked by building a blueprint projector. Found in the "Zerma - Docking Light" block category.
[*]Point the spotlight along the approach path, then set the line in the terminal.
[/list]

[h2]Known limitations[/h2]
[list]
[*]In-game texts are in French only.
[*]Not tested in game yet: please report any mod error with your log.
[/list]
