[h1]Full First Spawn[/h1]

New players start ready to go: on each player's [b]first spawn[/b] in the world, this mod fills [b]health, hunger, suit energy, oxygen and hydrogen[/b] to the maximum, and adds a [b]starter kit[/b] on top of the default tools: an S-10 pistol with 2 magazines, a flare gun and 5 flares.

[h2]Description[/h2]
[list]
[*]Applied once per player, about 2 seconds after the first spawn.
[*]A respawn after death is not refilled.
[*]The list of players already served is saved in the world, so it survives server restarts.
[*]Players who already spawned before the mod was added are served on their next spawn.
[/list]

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Add the mod to your world or to your dedicated server's mod list. No configuration.
[*]The script runs on the server (or on the host in single player).
[/list]

[h2]Known limitations[/h2]
[list]
[*]Not tested in game yet: please report any mod error with your log.
[/list]
