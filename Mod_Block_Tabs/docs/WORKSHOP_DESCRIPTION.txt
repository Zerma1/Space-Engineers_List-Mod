[h1]Mod Block Tabs[/h1]

Find modded blocks instantly: this mod adds [b]one tab per loaded mod[/b] to the build menu (G), named [b][MOD] <mod name>[/b], listing every block of that mod.

[table]
[tr][td][b]Version[/b][/td][td]1.0[/td][/tr]
[tr][td][b]Tested on Space Engineers[/b][/td][td]not tested in game yet[/td][/tr]
[tr][td][b]Last update[/b][/td][td]2026-10-30[/td][/tr]
[/table]

[h2]Description[/h2]
[list]
[*]Tabs are created automatically when the world loads, from the world's mod list.
[*]Blocks also stay in their original tabs: nothing is moved or removed.
[*]Works with any mod that adds blocks. No configuration.
[/list]

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Add the mod to your world (single player or dedicated server), then open the G menu: the [b][MOD] …[/b] tabs are listed with the other categories, in alphabetical order.
[*]The script only runs on the player's game; on a dedicated server it does nothing but is downloaded by every player who joins.
[/list]

[h2]Known limitations[/h2]
[list]
[*]Only public blocks are listed; the game still applies its usual filters (survival, research, DLC).
[*]Not tested in game yet: please report any "Prohibited type" or mod error with your log.
[/list]
